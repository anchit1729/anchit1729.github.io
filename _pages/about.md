I am a PhD student at the University of Waterloo, working in Human-Computer Interaction under the supervision of [Profs. Oliver Schneider](http://oliverschneider.ca) and [Matthew Brehmer](http://mattbrehmer.ca). My primary research interests include exploring the use of multimodal interactions in the context of mixed-reality applications. This work often involves the use of techniques from machine learning, computer graphics, and haptics, along with both qualitative and quantitative empirical research and analysis methods common in HCI. 

Outside of research, I enjoy playing around with rendering techniques, physics-based animation and other, more general visualizations. I'm also a big math-head, and like to solve problems in combinatorics, probability and algebra. 

My other interests include running/lifting, playing video games, playing the piano and listening to jazz, hip-hop and R&B music.
